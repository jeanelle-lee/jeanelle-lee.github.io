Wait point

Richel has requested a seat but the host has yet to confirm. Make the reservation status visible.

Failure points

A search can return no matching host. After booking, a late or cancelled pickup calls for a timely alert and a backup match.

Proposed service design for the OnRoute prototype. Operations and support processes are part of the concept.
