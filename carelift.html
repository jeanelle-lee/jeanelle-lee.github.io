CareLift

CareLift connects local pharmacies with licensed drone operators so rural patients who can’t drive can request a prescription delivery from home.

A refill in rural Michigan02
The problem03

How can Margaret get her refill without a ride?

Margaret lives alone in rural Michigan and can no longer drive. Even when her prescription is ready, she has to find someone to take her to the pharmacy.

Other times pickup is hard
A younger adult working a long shift may have little time to wait at the counter. A traveler who left medication at home may need a refill while visiting another state.
Finding work as a drone operator
Drone companies have capacity to deliver, but finding a job means contacting pharmacies or patients individually. Agreeing on a pickup takes more back-and-forth.
The delivery network04
Watch Margaret’s delivery story
Product decision05

One order, three views.

We designed a B2B2C model. The pharmacy is the drone operator’s business customer. By accepting its orders directly through CareLift, the operator delivers to patients like Margaret without having to find each patient individually.

  1. Patient viewRequest a refill and track its delivery in large type.
  2. Pharmacy viewVerify the prescription, check stock and mark the package ready for pickup.
  3. Operator viewAccept the pharmacy’s order, collect the package and share flight updates.
What comes next06

Prove the handoff in one town.

The team built a working three-app MVP during the six-week project. The proposed Michigan pilot still needs live pharmacy inventory and an operating partner.

First check
Ask rural pharmacists, older patients, and caregivers to try the order flow. Observe where they need to call for help.
Pilot measure
Track completed handoffs and request-to-door time. The 10–20 minute delivery window was a pitch target.
