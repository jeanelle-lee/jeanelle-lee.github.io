A refill in rural Michigan02
The problem03

Who has my prescription?

Margaret needs two answers before she can plan her day. Does the pharmacy have her refill, and when can an operator deliver it?

Patient outcome
See whether a refill can reach home and track its progress.
Our role
As the Care Pilots product team, we designed the handoffs between three apps. The pharmacy verifies and dispenses the medicine; a licensed partner operates the flight.
Anatomy of a handoff04
Product decision05

One order, three views.

Each handoff updates the next person in line, so Margaret can track her refill without making another call.

  1. Patient viewRequest a refill; check the delivery status in large type.
  2. Pharmacy viewVerify the prescription and stock before releasing the order.
  3. Operator viewSee ready orders, accept a job, and update the flight status.
What comes next06

Prove the handoff in one town.

The team built a working three-app MVP during the six-week project. The proposed Michigan pilot still needs live pharmacy inventory and an operating partner.

First check
Ask rural pharmacists, older patients, and caregivers to try the order flow. Observe where they need to call for help.
Pilot measure
Track completed handoffs and request-to-door time. The 10–20 minute delivery window was a pitch target.
