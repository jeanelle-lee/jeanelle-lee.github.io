OnRoute · Product discovery

Customer journey map

Jeannelle Lee · Double AJ

Rushing Richel is a U-M graduate student who lives off campus without a car. She needs a guaranteed seat for a 9 a.m. pre-exam review, an upfront price below ride-hail, and a verified student host.

Stuck

At 8:20, Richel watches two full buses pass. A solo ride costs too much.

Discover

A flyer or GroupMe link leads to OnRoute. She signs up with her U-M email and adds $20 to her wallet.

Book

She wants to arrive by 8:50 and walk at most 10 minutes. She compares three hosts and their ratings, reserves a $1.29 seat and waits for confirmation.

Ride

A possible cancellation makes the wait uneasy. A reminder guides her on a six-minute walk. She shares her trip, arrives ten minutes early and pays from her wallet.

Repeat

She rates the host, buys a Semester Pass and books the same Tuesday and Thursday commute.

Design opportunities

Place QR codes at busy stops. Show verification and on-time rates before booking. Alert riders to delays and offer a backup host. Make repeat trips easy to reserve.
